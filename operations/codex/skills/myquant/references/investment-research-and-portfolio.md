# Investment Research and Portfolio Framework

Use this reference for candidate discovery, company research, portfolio review,
paper evaluation, and learning. Infrastructure matters only when it changes the
credibility or actionability of the investment conclusion.

## Investment objective

Optimize expected portfolio return after drawdown, liquidity, concentration,
turnover, valuation, evidence, and execution costs. Every conclusion should
answer:

- What is the opportunity, and why might the market be wrong or slow?
- What confirms or contradicts the thesis?
- What catalyst or path can realize value, and what invalidates the thesis?
- What is already reflected in valuation?
- How should the idea interact with current holdings and cash?
- What future outcome would change the process?

## Candidate discovery

Start from the exact point-in-time investable A-share universe. Use a verified
system generation, governed admitted factor set, explicit pool policy, and
deterministic ranking. Combine price/volume, quality, growth, valuation,
revisions, capital efficiency, liquidity, tradability, robustness, incremental
portfolio value, costs, and capacity.

Diagnostic factor signals and broad mining output are not stock candidates. If
no formal current candidate list exists, produce a clearly labeled research
shortlist and state what remains unverified.

## Company and evidence research

For each selected company, examine business model, earnings drivers, industry
structure, competitive advantage, management, governance, capital allocation,
cash conversion, expectations, valuation scenarios, catalysts, balance sheet,
cyclicality, policy, customers, suppliers, technology, execution risk, evidence
freshness, provenance, and contradictions.

Separate facts, derived metrics, forecasts, claims, and judgments. Do not let
narrative confidence exceed evidence quality. Missing components remain
missing; a model cannot invent a score or source identity.

## Research responsibilities

- **Quant:** exact full-market pool and deterministic ranking from admitted
  factors.
- **Industry:** effective source-bound industry identity and component evidence.
- **Theme:** effective membership, exposure, lifecycle, and concentration risk.
- **Fundamental:** business quality, earnings, valuation, catalysts, and risk.
- **Macro/regime:** cash, gross exposure, risk budget, crowding tolerance, and
  vetoes; never company alpha.
- **Decision:** exact evidence closure, deterministic risk, vetoes, state, memo,
  and discipline evidence.
- **Skeptical review:** contrary evidence and bounded advisory challenge without
  decision authority.
- **Portfolio/paper:** constrained research portfolio, paper execution, matured
  outcomes, and learning without broker authority.

Record disagreements rather than averaging them away.

## Portfolio construction

Start with exact actual positions and cash. Assess expected return, downside,
confidence, evidence freshness, liquidity, capacity, factor and Industry/Theme
concentration, correlation, turnover, cost, and opportunity cost.

State what stays, is added, reduced, exited, or watched. Give sizing logic,
cash/gross rationale, vetoes, sequencing constraints, required evidence, and
invalidation conditions. A non-admitted current position may be reduced but not
increased through a governed research constructor.

An official target portfolio requires a valid active closure. A research
portfolio cannot mutate the effective holdings ledger.

## Factor and paper learning

Require economically interpretable, incremental value. Check point-in-time
integrity, tradability, neutralization, multiple testing, purged or embargoed
validation, regime stability, slippage, capacity, and prospective evidence.

Keep the sequence distinct:

```text
factor idea -> bootstrap definition -> preregistration -> prospective evidence
  -> admitted factor set -> stock candidate -> research portfolio
```

Paper scenarios, orders, fills, and broker execution are different facts. Bind
effective-dated calendar, T+1, lot, suspension, price-limit, IPO, price, partial
fill, fee, corporate-action, expiry, and cancellation assumptions. Missing
semantics remain blocked.

At matured horizons, evaluate benchmark-relative returns, realized PNL,
cost-adjusted excess return, drawdown, avoided loss, opportunity cost,
replacement alpha, turnover, slippage, regime stability, and gate quality.
Learning proposals never auto-change factors, risk budgets, sizing rules,
Memory, portfolios, or active state.

## Output

Lead with a concise investment verdict. For portfolios and candidates, use:

```text
代码 公司名 | 当前角色 | 结论 | 核心逻辑 | 主要风险 | 触发/失效条件
```

Then summarize exposure, concentration, cash, candidate quality, evidence
status, and next investment actions. Keep operational blockers short unless
they directly change the conclusion.
